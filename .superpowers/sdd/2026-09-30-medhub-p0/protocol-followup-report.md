GET fetches solely the selected exact catalog HTTPS `diseases.medelement.com/disease/...` URL, refuses redirects, caps response at 2 MB/7 seconds, requires HTML, and checks the page's own near-title `Версия: Клинические протоколы МЗ РК - YEAR (Казахстан)` metadata. It extracts at most six short literal rows from that page's `DIAGNOSTICS` section, discards script/style text, maps only broad field-presence categories, and flags every criterion for manual review. No patient payload, AI request, remote markup rendering, 3,206-page scrape, or claim of clinical compliance/current validity is involved. The catalog is read-only-mounted into Docker; `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` is also configured for the separate QR feature with a localhost-only default.
